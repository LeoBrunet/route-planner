Before you trust a line on the map

  1. Check the satellite image. Lantmäteriet's Min karta has recent orthophotos and the official topo map, which marks small forest roads.
  2. Check the heatmap. If the Strava ride heatmap shows traffic, the road goes through. No traffic on a long track often means a gate or a dead end.
  3. Look for logging. Fresh clear-cuts can turn a forest road into churned mud. Skogsstyrelsen's Skogens pärlor map shows notified fellings (avverkningsanmälningar).
  4. Respect barriers. Boom gates (bom) on private roads stop cars, not bikes. “Enskild väg” is fine to ride; “Privat” near houses is not.

Seasons and conditions

Right now: moose hunting.The moose hunt in southern Sweden runs from mid-October through the winter, mostly on weekday mornings and weekends. Wear orange or a bright colour in the forest and stay on roads.
  • Autumn and spring mud.Naturvårdsverket asks riders to avoid wet, muddy trails, which tear up fast. Stick to fast-gravel roads from November to April.
  • Spring thaw (tjällossning), March–April.Forest roads go soft as the frost leaves. Some get weight-limit signs. On a bike it means slow, deep gravel.
  • Best months.Late May to early October. Granite trails dry within a day or two of rain; clay valleys along Säveån and Göta älv stay sticky.
  • Daylight.In late October the sun sets around 17:00. Bring lights for anything over 3 hours.

Making your own GPX

Planners that show surfaces

  • BRouter-webFree, open source, the one used here. Pick the “Gravel” profile, click points, open the Analysis tab for a surface breakdown, export GPX. You can tweak the profile to avoid paths.
  • Komoot“Gravel ride” sport type with a surface and way-type breakdown on every route. Region pack needed for offline; easy sync to Garmin and Wahoo.
  • Ride with GPSSurface overlay on the map and an unpaved percentage per route. Dalsland's official gravel routes are published here.
  • GraphHopper MapsShows surface, road class and steepness as coloured bands along the route profile. Exports GPX.
  • Mapy.comGood free planner with a cycling map that marks unpaved roads clearly, plus offline maps on the phone.
  • CyclOSMA map style, not a planner. Draws unpaved roads dashed so you can scan an area quickly.

Script on your Mac

The loops above came from a small script in route-planner/scripts/. Give it waypoints and it writes a GPX file with elevation and prints how many km are paved, fast gravel, rough or technical.

cd ~/Documents/DEV/route-planner
python3 scripts/route.py "My loop" \
  "57.7705,12.2690;57.8287,12.1398;57.7705,12.2690"

Tip for gravel routing: put waypoints in the middle of the gravel roads you want, not at junctions. scripts/longways.py lists the longest gravel roads inside any box (south, west, north, east).

python3 scripts/longways.py 57.74 12.25 57.85 12.5

Use a profile other than gravel (for example trekking) as a third argument for faster, more paved routes.

Routes and rules

Official and signed routes

  • Dalsland gravel17 loops with GPX on Ride with GPS and over 850 km of gravel roads. About an hour by train from Göteborg. The best gravel weekend in the region.
  • KattegattledenNational route 1, Göteborg to Helsingborg, 390 km in 8 stages. Mostly asphalt with gravel sections, signed and with GPX.
  • Sverigeleden and VästgötaledenFollow Göta älv north through Lilla Edet to Trollhättan and Vänersborg. Mixed surfaces; good for a one-way ride and a train home.
  • Sjuhäradsrundan150 km loop around Borås, 120 km car-free on old railway beds. Svenljunga–Borås has about 8 km of gravel.
  • VänerledenRound lake Vänern from Vänersborg. A multi-day tour, mostly quiet roads.
  • Naturkartan: Göteborg cyclingMunicipal nature areas, including the Delsjön loops and Änggårdsbergen MTB trails, with reserve rules.

Rules and logistics

  • Allemansrätten: cyclingYou may ride private roads and trails. Keep a distance from houses, avoid fields and wet ground, and get landowner permission for organised group rides or races.
  • E-bikes in the terrainNaturvårdsverket's view is that allemansrätten does not cover e-bikes, even 250 W pedelecs. Roads are fine; trails are a grey zone.
  • Västtrafik: bikes on boardFree on Västtåg and Öresundståg when there's space; staff decide. No bikes on trams, buses or replacement buses. SJ and Vy have their own rules.
  • Trafikverket NVDB (Lastkajen)The national road database, including surface type (slitlager) on public and many private roads. Useful to cross-check OSM.
  • Gravel Grit ’n Grind, HalmstadThe nearest big gravel event, a UCI Gravel World Series qualifier, two hours south.
